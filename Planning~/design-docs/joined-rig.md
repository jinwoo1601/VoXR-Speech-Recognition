# Design: Joining the Push-Audio Harness to the Parser Rig

**Branch:** `design-joined-rig`
**Origin:** next-level plan item R1 (`03-critique-and-ranking.md` rank 4; Phase C).
**Status:** In design — opened at G0 2026-10-13; not locked.

**Scope, agreed at G0 (2026-10-13):** join the WSL push-audio harness (real audio through the real decoder) to the parser A/B rig (the real parser) with a pipe, so one run goes audio → decoder → parser → scored outcome; replace the exact-equality comparison at `VoxrBatchTestRunner.cs:230-233` with a scorer that gives slot partial credit; define a run-output format that the gated items consume; state how the standing rule "re-pin, never edit" (rulings 2 and 10) applies to the acoustic baselines, so that `expectations.json` and the WAV-replay expectations are regenerated by running the harness and never hand-edited; and have the rig accept the manifests of the future R5 human corpus and R4 negative corpus without building either. The kill criterion: if the joined rig cannot reproduce the harness's committed `expectations.json` baseline byte for byte on the 16 fixtures, the plumbing is wrong and no number from it may be quoted.

> **What's decided going in.** The human (the maintainer) ruled at G0 on 2026-10-13: the work runs on the **design track**, branch `design-joined-rig`, with the scope above agreed as proposed. The item is needed because the two instruments already exist but the pipe between them and a scorer with slot partial credit do not: the push-audio harness already replays real audio through the real decoder (`NativeBridge~/harness/main.cpp:1-8`), the parser rig already stages the real parser (`stage.sh:15-25`), and the current comparison is exact equality (`VoxrBatchTestRunner.cs:230-233`). It gates R10, R11, R12, R13, R14's acceptance, R21, R22 and both R41 screens — everything decoder-, grammar- or DSP-side. The kill criterion: if the joined rig cannot reproduce the harness's committed `expectations.json` baseline byte for byte on the 16 fixtures, the plumbing is wrong and no number from it may be quoted.
>
> **Non-goals:** any decoder, DSP or grammar change (R11, R14, R15 and the rest are later items gated on this one); building the R5 human corpus or the R4 negative corpus; the Phase B probes (a separate lab, run concurrently as `lab-next-level-probes`); ranking two decoders on TTS-only audio (standing rule, critique C12) (see §0).

## 0. Scope (agreed at G0)

**In**

1. The pipe from the WSL push-audio harness (real audio → real decoder) into the parser A/B rig (real parser), so one run goes audio → decoder → parser → scored outcome.
2. A scorer with slot partial credit, replacing the exact-equality comparison at `VoxrBatchTestRunner.cs:230-233`.
3. A run-output format that the gated items consume: R10, R11, R12, R13, R14's acceptance, R21, R22 and both R41 screens.
4. How the standing rule "re-pin, never edit" (rulings 2 and 10) applies to the acoustic baselines: `expectations.json` and the WAV-replay expectations are regenerated by running the harness, never hand-edited.
5. The rig accepts the manifests of the future R5 human corpus and R4 negative corpus, without building either.

**Kill criterion**

- If the joined rig cannot reproduce the harness's committed `expectations.json` baseline byte for byte on the 16 fixtures, the plumbing is wrong and no number from it may be quoted.
- Cheapest first experiment: run the existing harness and the existing rig on the same fixture and diff the transcript each believes it parsed.

**Out**

- Any decoder, DSP or grammar change (R11, R14, R15 and the rest are later items gated on this one).
- Building the R5 human corpus or the R4 negative corpus.
- The Phase B probes (a separate lab).
- Ranking two decoders on TTS-only audio (standing rule, critique C12).

**Gates**

- R10, R11, R12, R13, R14's acceptance, R21, R22, and both R41 screens.
