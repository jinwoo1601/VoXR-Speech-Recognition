# Steps that take no judgment

The constitution's *Every invariant names its enforcer* puts prose "only where it takes judgment". This reference applies that clause to the steps of skills and references (design `no-judgment-scripts`, `adr-0503`): a step its inputs fully determine is a script's to run, not the model's. Each rule below is held by the enforcer `## The enforcers` names. The rule as a whole is advice: a step still run in prose fails no check, as its row in `.claude/references/core/constitution-annex.md` `## Invariants and their enforcers` says.

## The rules

1. **The test.** A step takes no judgment when its inputs — files, command output, binding slots — fully determine its output by a rule the prose already states. Such a step runs as a verb or a flag on a base script (`scripts/hub.py`, `scripts/harness.py`, `scripts/run_tests.py`). A step with a judgment part is split: the verb does the mechanical part and takes the judgment as an argument the model passes, never inferring it — release versus decline of an item, `kept both` versus `chose`, the human's ruling words, a halt's free-text note. Judgment, gates, human stops, wording for people, permission refusals and the Bash timeout stay with the model. *Source:* design `no-judgment-scripts`, N1.
2. **The shape.** One step is one command, alone in its call, under its script's existing allow rule; no new allow rule is added. It prints result lines that open with a fixed first word. Where the skill relays a line, the verb prints it ready to relay: the predecessor suffix, a close-tree outcome line, `?` for non-ASCII. Refusals keep `scripts/hub.py`'s conventions: one stderr line, exit 1, `correct:`, `re-run:`, `next:`, `clear:`. A verb that writes `memory/` does so inside `hub.py write`'s one-lock, one-commit operation. A verb whose act is irreversible, a cut or a removal, has a `--check` form that reads and refuses with no act. *Source:* design `no-judgment-scripts`, N2.
3. **Slots, the keystone.** The verb runs the version-control form the backend classes of `scripts/hub.py` code, the same form the pack's binding template names. A pack whose step is a prose probe ships a script for it. Where the project's slot does not read as its pack's template default, or the *Path form* is not the Windows form the scripts compare by, the verb does nothing and prints `manual: <slot>`; the skill then runs that slot's prose step as the binding writes it. An unfilled or absent binding keeps today's behaviour, as elsewhere in the harness. *Source:* design `no-judgment-scripts`, N3; `adr-0502`.
4. **Placement.** New code goes in its own modules beside the script it extends; `scripts/hub.py` and `scripts/harness.py` gain parser entries and dispatch only, and tests go in their own files. The verbs read bindings through the existing readers — `scripts/hub.py`'s `trees_pair`, `list_sessions`, `main_branch` and its backend classes — and no fourth parser is added. *Source:* design `no-judgment-scripts`, N4.
5. **The prose.** Once a step is a verb, the skill or reference text shrinks to the call plus an outcome table, one row per first word, saying what the model does next. The rules move into the verb's `--help` and its tests. The outcome table's `manual:` row tells the model to run the named slot as the binding writes it; the verb does every other part of the step, so a customised slot costs the model only that slot's part. Locked design docs that describe prose steps are records, and stay as they are. *Source:* design `no-judgment-scripts`, N5; `adr-0505`.

## The enforcers

| Rule | Enforcer |
|---|---|
| A step a verb performs, rules 1 to 5 as that verb applies them. | The verb's `--help` and its tests in `scripts/tests/`, its `manual:` output included. |
| A step whose rule is structural. | The validator, where a check is structural; none yet. |
| New skill or reference prose: a step that takes no judgment, which no verb performs, or one that re-derives what a verb already prints. | `review-cycle`'s CONV angle, `.claude/references/core/angle-roster.md` `### CONV — conventions`, on the full and prose profiles that `review-cycle` `## Depth profiles` runs it on by default; advice. |
| The rule's register. | Its row in `.claude/references/core/constitution-annex.md` `## Invariants and their enforcers`, whose upkeep nothing checks. |
