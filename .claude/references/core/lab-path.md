# The lab path

The constitution's **Paths** paragraph has a path's pack hold its stages. These are the lab path's, binding on every piece of work on it, each held by the enforcer `## The enforcers` names.

## The rules

An experiment runs on a `lab-<topic>` branch with no closing gate. It writes only under the scratch area — the PM may write its probes there itself — and merges nothing; its facts enter canon only through a recon record written on the consuming branch, under that branch's G1 or G2.

Its G0 is the human's word at the hub that names the experiment, recorded with the path.
